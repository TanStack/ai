Add typed headless `createChatUI()` adapters on `@tanstack/ai-react/ui`, `@tanstack/ai-solid/ui`, `@tanstack/ai-vue/ui`, and `@tanstack/ai-svelte/ui`. Chat options control the types of message parts, tools, structured output, and interrupts.

`@tanstack/ai-react-ui`, `@tanstack/ai-solid-ui`, and `@tanstack/ai-vue-ui` are deprecated. They re-export the new `/ui` subpath until 1.0.0. `@tanstack/ai-svelte-ui` was never published.

Old Chat orchestration stays importable and deprecated until 1.0.
